# Echoes Smoke Checklist

1. Start a local server (e.g., `python -m http.server 8000`).
2. Open `http://localhost:8000/index.html?seed=1`.
3. Confirm the HUD renders and seed badge is visible.
4. Click **Roll** to generate dice values.
5. Select an action with a highlighted number and perform it.
6. Move a fleet into an unrevealed hex and confirm a card modal appears.
7. Resolve a card choice; confirm token placement and log output.
8. Move into an enemy-occupied hex to trigger combat.
9. Roll a combat round and confirm hits/damage apply.
10. Retreat or end combat; confirm the modal closes and turn resumes.
